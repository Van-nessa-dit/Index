Our Story

Who We Are

Founded in 2020, Green Harvest Agriculture has been at the forefront of sustainable farming innovation. We believe that healthy food starts with healthy soil and empowered farmers.

Our team of agronomists, educators, and field experts work side by side with local communities to increase yields while preserving natural resources.

We are proud to support over 500 smallholder farmers across the region, providing training, access to markets, and climate‑resilient practices.
